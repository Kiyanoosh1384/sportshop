# 🏆 SportPro - Professional Sports Shop Template

A fully responsive, customizable HTML/CSS/JS template for sports equipment stores.

## 🔥 Features
- ✅ 14+ Complete Pages
- ✅ Fully Responsive Design
- ✅ Modern & Clean UI
- ✅ 78 Sports Categories
- ✅ Shopping Cart System
- ✅ Wishlist Functionality
- ✅ Advanced Search
- ✅ Product Filtering & Sorting
- ✅ Blog with 6 Articles
- ✅ Contact Form
- ✅ Google Maps Integration
- ✅ Pure HTML/CSS/JS (No Frameworks)

## 📁 Pages
1. Home Page
2. Shop (78 Sports)
3. Sports Products
4. Product Detail
5. Shopping Cart
6. Checkout
7. About Us
8. Contact Us
9. Blog
10. Single Post
11. Account
12. Wishlist
13. 404 Error

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- HTML5
- CSS3 (Variables, Flexbox, Grid)
- JavaScript (ES6)
- Font Awesome 6
- LocalStorage for Cart & Wishlist

## 📥 Installation
1. Download the template
2. Open `template/pages/index.html` in your browser
3. Customize as needed!
